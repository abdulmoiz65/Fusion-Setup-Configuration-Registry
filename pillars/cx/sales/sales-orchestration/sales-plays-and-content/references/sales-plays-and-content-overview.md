# Sales Plays and Content Overview

## Purpose

Use this reference for source-backed conceptual and business-value questions
about Sales Plays and the collateral they contain. Use the task's BOSS
reference for `SALES_PLAY` and `CONTENT_BUNDLE` record metadata and use the
Content Intelligence references for semantic-search behavior.

## What a Sales Play Is

A Sales Play is used within a Sales Motion to make day-to-day sales activities
repeatable, measurable, and automatable. It contains collateral for selling
specific products or product groups, which sellers use in presentations and
customer communications.

## How It Helps

The agent uses Sales Play collateral during the sales cycle to generate
talking points, draft emails, and recommendations. This makes approved selling
knowledge available in the flow of seller work. When the sales process varies
by industry or market segment, Oracle recommends creating separate Sales Plays
for those differing processes.

## Content and Type Boundary

- In the public overview, "collateral" describes the selling material contained
  by a Sales Play.
- The public page does not define a content bundle or packet as a separate
  record type.
- For this task, use the existing BOSS contract to distinguish `SALES_PLAY`
  from `CONTENT_BUNDLE` by the `salesPlayType` attribute. Do not derive that
  record-type contract from the public overview.

  authoring, end-user access, published-content filters, and search results.
