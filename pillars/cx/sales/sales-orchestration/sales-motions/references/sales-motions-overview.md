# Sales Motions Overview

## Purpose

Use this reference for source-backed conceptual and business-value questions
about Sales Motions. Use `sales-motions-boss-read.md` for object metadata and
read API behavior, and `sales-motions-security.md` for security prerequisites.

## What a Sales Motion Is

A Sales Motion defines a sales strategy for a customer segment, product set,
or selling scenario. It gives the sales organization a repeatable workflow and
the steps needed to achieve a specific sales objective.

## How It Helps

Sales Motions capture an organization's strategies for different product
classes and market segments. They align sellers to those strategies and
provide a framework intended to improve the likelihood of closing a deal.

When creating a Sales Motion with agent assistance, an organization can:

- capture and refine its sales best practices;
- generate objectives and the high-value activities sellers can perform to
  achieve those objectives; and
- define criteria used by the agent to evaluate an opportunity and determine
  whether objectives have been met.

As the sale progresses, sellers and managers can see whether objectives and
their suggested activities are pending, in progress, or complete.

## Design Considerations

- Different strategies or processes can justify different Sales Motions, such
  as equipment versus software sales or private-sector versus public-sector
  selling.
- Oracle states that many organizations need only about half a dozen Sales
  Motions. Treat this as planning guidance, not a product limit or validation
  rule.
- Do not infer Sales Motion record statuses, field names, REST payloads, setup
  validations, or activation transitions from this business overview. Use the
  target-release BOSS contract or return `SOURCE_GAP`.
